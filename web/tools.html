    Methods and model limits

    • Stoichiometry: integer atom conservation, coefficient ratios, limiting extent and percent yield.
    • Gases: PV=nRT, pᵢ=xᵢP, Graham ratio and user-supplied van der Waals a/b.
    • Solutions: molarity/solution liters, molality/solvent kilograms, C1V1=C2V2, |ΔT|=iKm.
    • Acid–base: pH=−log10 aH+, Kw≈1e−14 at 25°C; only supported monoprotic ideal models.
    • Equilibrium: Cᵢ=Cᵢ,initial+νᵢξ; solve Σνᵢln activity=ln K. K is not general odds.
    • Math: bounded real AST, linear/quadratic polynomials, elementary derivatives and polynomial-only antiderivatives. Trig arguments are radians.
    • Units: kg/m/s/mol/K dimensions, exact supported conversions. No guessed missing units or affine Celsius compounds.

    Offline works after the app assets have been cached during an online visit. AI, shared-board saving/opening and uncached source websites need a connection. No failed writes are silently queued.
