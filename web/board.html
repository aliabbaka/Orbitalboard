Your energy diagram

One orbital per line, low → high. Format: 1: ↑↓ or 2:. You can reorder the numbered orbitals. Pauli permits at most one ↑ and one ↓ per level. Degenerate order swaps are accepted.

Save a shared board

Anyone with the public link can view. Only the separate owner token can update or delete it. Sharing sends the board and pins to this server; local saving does not. No accounts. Stored boards remain until owner/operator deletion. Keep a JSON backup.

Nothing leaves your device until Save shared board is pressed. Loopback links work on this machine only; deploy the app to share across devices.

Export your work

Snapshots include source versions and alt text. Numerical values re-open from the pinned solver; uploaded image pixels are attachments, not verified scientific data. Unknown solver/schema versions are rejected, never silently upgraded.
